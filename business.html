Spending by category

Budget versus actual

Full planned targets with activity inside the selected dates and budget period. Targets are not prorated or added together.

Export or print

Original currencies are never added together. Reporting values use stored conversion snapshots in —. Actuals use income and company-payment dates in the workspace timezone. Unpaid commitments use expense, planned-purchase or bill-due dates and show current balances. Claim-register values include every status and are separate from company payments. Missing-document checks cover expense receipts, supplier invoices and individual payment proofs. These are recorded figures, not profit or a bank balance.
