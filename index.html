From due date to payment record

  1. 1

    Add your bill

    Enter the amount, due date, and how often the bill repeats. Set a reminder for when you want to act.

  2. 2

    Pay through your provider

    Use your bank, mobile money app, or the biller’s own payment service to pay the bill.

  3. 3

    Record what you paid

    Choose Record payment in Mushavo Budget. Enter the full or partial amount and date, and attach a receipt if you have one.
